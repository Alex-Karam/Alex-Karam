# Alexandros Karam

Theoretical physicist based in Tallinn, Estonia, transitioning into data analytics.

My research background includes mathematical modelling, numerical analysis and communicating complex results. I’m building practical analytics experience through projects using MySQL, Excel / Power Query, Python and Power BI.

## Data analytics projects

- **[Customer Activation and Feature Adoption](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/customer-activation/index.html)**  
  Funnels, conversion windows, cohorts and repeat activity using MySQL and Power BI. Completed analysis with a published case study and three-page Power BI report available as a PDF. Uses 365 Data Science student observations under an explicitly hypothetical financial-services interpretation. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/customer-activation-sql-power-bi)

- **[Small Business Sales and Order Analytics](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/small-business-sales-and-order-analytics/index.html)**  
  Turning operational spreadsheets into a validated MySQL model and a completed four-page Power BI report. Published case study using approved real aggregate business results. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/small-business-sales-and-order-analytics)

- **[Cookie Cats A/B Test](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/cookie-cats-ab-test/index.html)**  
  Analysed day-7 retention for 90,189 players using MySQL, Python and Power BI. Moving the progression gate from level 30 to level 40 was associated with a 0.82 percentage-point drop in day-7 retention (95% CI: −1.33 to −0.31). Recommended keeping level 30 while verifying the planned allocation and day-7 follow-up coverage. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/cookie-cats-ab-test)

- **[ASOS Decision Timing](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/asos-decision-timing/index.html)**  
  Replayed three A/B test stopping rules across 37 historical ASOS comparisons using Python and Power BI. Repeated 5% testing reached 15 early decisions, compared with 10 under Bonferroni correction. The final readout served as a reference because the true effects are unknown. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/asos-decision-timing)

- **[Credit Card Repayment Risk](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/credit-card-repayment-risk/index.html)**  
  Historical study of next-month default among 30,000 Taiwanese credit-card clients from 2005, using MySQL, Python, Power BI and Excel. On 6,000 held-out clients, logistic regression achieved ROC AUC of 0.7719 versus 0.5000 for the baseline. A hypothetical queue reviewing the top 20% captured 673 of 1,327 observed defaults (50.72%). [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/credit-card-repayment-risk)

- **[KYC Review and Capacity Planning](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/kyc-review-capacity-planning/index.html)**  
  Built a MySQL follow-up queue for fictional customer files and used Python to forecast onboarding and simulate staffing. Adding one analyst during a five-day surge raised simulated on-time completion from 83.0% to 100%. The two-page Power BI report shows the queue and staffing results. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/kyc-review-capacity-planning)

- **[AML Transaction Review](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/aml-transaction-review/index.html)**  
  Compared three rankings on IBM synthetic transactions using MySQL, Python and Power BI, freezing the rankings before accessing the test labels. On 9–10 September 2022, at 500 reviews per ranking per day, the decision tree caught 53 of 956 transactions labelled as laundering. Amount alone caught 7 and the weighted history rule caught 1. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/aml-transaction-review)

- **[Merchant Payments Reconciliation](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/merchant-payments-reconciliation/index.html)**  
  Reconciled synthetic EUR ledger, processor and bank records in MySQL. At the 30 June cutoff, €159.00 of €422.00 had posted. All three payouts matched bank postings by 2 July, with four ledger and processor exceptions still open. All 42 dbt tests passed, and the two-page Power BI report shows both cutoffs. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/merchant-payments-reconciliation)

- **[FX Time Range Breakout — Backtest Analytics](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/fx-time-range-breakout/index.html)**  
  Python/pandas validation and MySQL analysis of historical breakout configurations across 28 FX pairs, with a separate USDJPY exit-time study. Completed 14-page Power BI report; findings are in-sample research results. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/fx-time-range-breakout)

- **[QQQ Options Backtest Reconciliation and Risk Analysis](https://alex-karam.github.io/projects/qqq-options-backtest-reconciliation/index.html)**  
  Reconciled fees and net P&L for 415 simulated QQQ options trades and tied 830 orders to gross leg cash flows in MySQL. Cross-checked monthly totals in Python and recorded seven open settlement exceptions. The three-page Power BI report covers returns, drawdown and fill-cost sensitivity over a 100-month historical window. [Technical repository](https://github.com/Alex-Karam/qqq-options-backtest-reconciliation)
